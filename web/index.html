TUNE YOUR RADAR

Sources & settings

Start with 5–10 relevant people. Scans check your feed and these profiles' public activity. A large watchlist splits the available scan time.

No post-count limit. The runtime budget limits how far a scan gets; it never means all posts were covered.

Comma-separated. A post must mention a role keyword and hiring intent. U.S. locations and small-team signals boost ranking; they are not verified facts about eligibility.

Keep HireSay running and your computer awake. If you open it after the scheduled time, it attempts today's scan then. Failed scans can be retried manually.

Used only when you choose “Draft intro.” Templates are editable and never sent automatically.

Import LinkedIn's Connections.csv to label known first-degree authors. This does not give access to their connections or their home feeds.
